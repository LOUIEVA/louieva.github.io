Quote follow-up for tradies: win the jobs you already quoted

You drove out, measured up and wrote the quote. Then nothing. Most of the time the customer didn't say no, they just got busy. A simple follow-up sequence wins a lot of those jobs back, and it can run without you.

By Louie Dela Cruz, HighLevel Certified Admin · 9 November 2026

Why quotes go quiet

  • The customer is getting two or three quotes and hasn't decided.
  • They meant to reply, then life got in the way.
  • They have a question but don't want to ring and feel pressured.
  • The tradie who followed up politely felt more reliable, so they got the job.

Following up isn't pushy. It shows you're organised, and that's exactly what people want from someone working on their home.

The sequence

This is the sequence I set up in GoHighLevel. It starts when you move the job to "Quote sent" and stops the moment the customer replies or books.

Same dayEmail with the quote, plus a short text: "Quote's in your inbox. Any questions, just reply here."
Day 2Text: "Hi [Name], did the quote make sense? Happy to explain anything."
Day 5Text with a reason to decide: "I'm booking jobs for [month]. Want me to hold a spot for you?"
Day 10Email with photos or reviews from a similar job.
Day 30Last text: "Should I close this one off, or would you like me to keep it open?"
Tip: the "should I close this off?" message often gets the most replies. People answer a yes or no question.

How to set it up in GoHighLevel

  1. Create a pipeline: New enquiry, Site visit booked, Quote sent, Won, Lost.
  2. Build a workflow triggered when an opportunity moves to "Quote sent".
  3. Add the texts and emails above with waits between them.
  4. Set the workflow to stop when the contact replies, books or the job is marked Won or Lost.
  5. Create a task for you on day 3 to make one quick call on bigger quotes.

What happens when they say yes

Move the job to Won and a second workflow takes over: confirmation text, booking reminder, and after the job, an automatic Google review request.

Questions

How many times should a tradie follow up a quote?

Four or five friendly touches over about a month works well, stopping as soon as the customer replies.

Is it pushy to follow up a quote?

Not if it's short and helpful. Most customers appreciate a tradie who is organised and easy to reach.

Can follow-ups stop automatically when the customer replies?

Yes. In GoHighLevel the workflow can stop when the contact replies, books or the job is marked won or lost.
