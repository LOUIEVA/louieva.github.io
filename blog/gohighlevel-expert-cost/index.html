How much does a GoHighLevel expert cost in 2026?

Short answer: anywhere from about $6 an hour for an offshore VA to a few thousand a month for an agency. The right choice depends on whether you need someone to build the system, run it, or both. Here's how the options compare, with real numbers.

The quick numbers

Here's what people charge in 2026, from published rate guides:

  • GoHighLevel VA in the Philippines: about $6 to $12 an hour, or $600 to $1,000 a month full time (DDIY rate guide).
  • Latin America: about $8 to $15 an hour. Eastern Europe: about $12 to $25.
  • US, Canada and UK based help: about $30 to $60 an hour or more.
  • Freelance GoHighLevel builders on marketplaces: often $25 to $100 an hour, or a flat $500 to $3,000 for a single build.
  • Agencies: retainers of $1,500 to $5,000 a month or more (Ad Snipper, which sells GoHighLevel staffing, so read it with that in mind).

Your GoHighLevel subscription is separate from all of these. So is ad spend, and SMS and call usage.

Hourly help: when it makes sense

An hourly VA is great once the system exists. Updating a workflow, building a new form, cleaning contacts, checking the inbox. You stay the manager, so you need to know what you want.

Where it goes wrong is the first build. A cheap hourly person learning on your account can take weeks, and you pay for every hour of it. I've cleaned up plenty of accounts where nothing was tested and leads were going nowhere.

Done-for-you: when it makes sense

If you don't have GoHighLevel set up yet, or it's a mess, a fixed-price build is usually cheaper in the end. You know the price before you start, and you get a working system, not a timesheet.

My own bundles, for example:

  • Launch: €1,190 / £990 / A$1,990 one-off. Website up to 5 pages, a landing page, CRM, pipeline, calendar, follow-up workflow and 30 days support.
  • Growth: €1,490 / £1,290 / A$2,490 setup, then €590 / £490 / A$990 a month. Adds ads management, tracking and more landing pages.
  • All In: adds AI lead scraping, outreach and an AI chat agent. Full details on the pricing section.

How to compare quotes

  1. Ask what's included. Setup only, or automation and testing too? Who writes the messages?
  2. Ask for a real example. A short video or case study of something they built. Here are mine.
  3. Ask who owns the account. It should be yours, or a sub-account you can move.
  4. Ask what happens after launch. Who fixes things when a form or integration breaks?
  5. Check the hours. If your leads come in during UK or Australian business hours, your help should be awake then too.
