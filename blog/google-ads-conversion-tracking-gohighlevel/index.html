How to track Google Ads conversions in GoHighLevel

If Google Ads shows more conversions than you have real leads, your campaigns are learning from the wrong thing. Here's how to fix it with GoHighLevel, step by step, using the method I used for One Om School of Yoga.

Why your Google Ads numbers don't match your CRM

Google's smart bidding optimises for whatever you tell it is a conversion. If that conversion fires on a thank-you page view, a button click or a form start, Google counts things that never become a real lead. It then spends your budget finding more of the same.

At One Om, Google Ads showed 330 conversions while GoHighLevel had 113 real leads. After the fix: 18 vs 17.

Step 1: Check the gap

Pick last month. Write down conversions in Google Ads. Count new leads from Google in GoHighLevel. If the numbers are far apart, keep going.

Step 2: Capture the GCLID and UTMs on every form

When someone clicks your ad, Google adds a gclid to the link. Add hidden fields for the GCLID and your UTM parameters (source, medium, campaign) to every GoHighLevel form and survey, and make sure auto-tagging is on in Google Ads. Each lead now carries the exact click and campaign it came from.

Check: submit a test form from a real ad click and confirm the GCLID is saved on the contact. If it's blank, the parameter is being dropped by a redirect or a page builder setting.

Step 3: Decide what a real conversion is

For most local businesses it's a real enquiry that lands in the CRM. Later you can add better ones: booked call, showed up, sale. Start with the one you can trust.

Step 4: Send real leads back to Google Ads

Create an offline (imported) conversion action in Google Ads. Then send each real lead back with its GCLID and the time it converted. You can do this with:

  • GoHighLevel's Google Ads connection, where available on your account,
  • a workflow webhook into an automation tool like n8n or Zapier that uploads the conversion, or
  • a scheduled conversion upload from a sheet for low volumes.

Step 5: Turn off the old conversion as primary

Once real conversions are flowing, set the old page-view conversion to secondary so bidding learns only from real leads. Give the campaigns time to relearn before judging them.

Step 6: Follow up fast so the leads actually convert

Tracking shows you which campaigns work. Follow-up makes them work. At One Om every lead gets an info packet by email, SMS, a WhatsApp voice note and a voicemail. Read why speed to lead matters so much.

Questions

What is offline conversion tracking?

It means telling Google Ads which clicks became real leads or sales after they happened, using the Google Click ID saved in your CRM.

Do I need a developer to set this up?

No. Hidden form fields, a GoHighLevel workflow and a conversion upload are enough for most small businesses.

How long before Google Ads improves?

Smart bidding needs time and conversion volume to relearn, so judge results over weeks, not days.
