Admin / Executive VA

Ikaw ang kanang kamay ng client: email, calendar, files at maliliit na tasks na kumakain ng oras niya.

Karaniwang tasks

  • Inbox at calendar management
  • Pag-book ng meetings at travel
  • Data entry at pag-ayos ng files
  • Paggawa ng docs, sheets at reports

Tools na gagamitin

  • Google Workspace: Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets
  • Microsoft 365: Outlook, Word, Excel
  • Calendly: Booking ng meetings
  • Password manager: Ligtas na access sa accounts

Paano matuto

  1. Aralin ang Gmail, Calendar at Drive
  2. Sanayin ang Docs at Sheets basics
  3. Gumawa ng sample na inbox at calendar system
  4. Ilagay sa portfolio ang SOP na ginawa mo

Libreng courses

Google Workspace Learning CenterLibreng training at tutorials ng Google para sa Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs at Sheets.TESDA Online ProgramLibreng online courses ng TESDA, may digital at entrepreneurship courses.HubSpot AcademyLibreng business at marketing courses na may certificate.

Practice project para sa portfolio: Gumawa ng sample na inbox labels at weekly calendar para sa isang pretend na client, tapos isulat ang SOP.

Panoorin din: VA 101 Ep 31: Inbox at Calendar Management, VA 101 Ep 34: Google Docs at Sheets sa YouTube.

Customer Support VA

Ikaw ang sumasagot sa customers ng client sa email, chat o tickets, nang mabilis at magalang.

Karaniwang tasks

  • Pagsagot sa emails at chat
  • Pag-handle ng tickets, refunds at complaints
  • Pag-update ng order at customer info
  • Paggawa ng saved replies at FAQ

Tools na gagamitin

  • Zendesk: Helpdesk at tickets
  • Gorgias o Freshdesk: Helpdesk para sa ecommerce
  • Gmail at chat: Shared inbox at live chat
  • Google Sheets: Tracking ng issues

Paano matuto

  1. Aralin ang customer service basics at tamang tono
  2. Kilalanin ang helpdesk: tickets, status, macros
  3. Mag-practice ng sagot sa galit na customer
  4. Gumawa ng sample na saved replies

Libreng courses
