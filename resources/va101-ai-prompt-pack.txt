VA 101 sa Tagalog: AI Prompt Pack for VAs
louieva.github.io/resources

SAFE AI RULES
1. Ask your client first if you can use AI on their work.
2. Never paste passwords, card or bank numbers, or customers' personal details.
3. Read and fact-check everything before you send it. You are responsible, not the AI.
4. Use AI to start faster, then make it sound like you.

=== 1. Email reply ===
Act as my writing assistant. I am a virtual assistant replying to an email for my client [client business]. Write a short, friendly and professional reply.
Goal of the reply: [what you want to say]
Tone: [warm / formal / casual]
Keep it under 120 words. Do not promise anything I did not mention.

Email I received:
[paste email, remove personal details]

=== 2. Make it clear and polite ===
Rewrite my message so it is clear, polite and easy to read. Keep my meaning. Use simple English. Give me 2 versions: one short, one a bit warmer.

My message:
[paste]

=== 3. SOP from my notes ===
Turn my notes into a step-by-step SOP (standard operating procedure).
Format: Title, Purpose, When to do it, Tools needed, numbered Steps, Done when, Common mistakes.
Use short steps that start with a verb. Ask me questions if a step is unclear.

My notes:
[paste notes or Loom transcript]

=== 4. Meeting notes to tasks ===
From these meeting notes, list every task as: Task, Owner, Due date, Notes.
If an owner or date is missing, write "ask" so I can confirm. Then write a 3-line summary I can send to the client.

Notes:
[paste]

=== 5. Plan my day ===
Here are my tasks for today and my shift length of [hours] hours. Sort them by urgency and importance, estimate the time for each, and give me a simple schedule with short breaks. Flag anything I should ask my client about.

Tasks:
[list]

=== 6. Research summary ===
Research [topic] for my client. Give me: a 5-bullet summary, 3 options compared in a table (price, pros, cons), and your recommendation. List the sources you used so I can check them. If you are not sure about something, say so.

=== 7. Social media captions ===
Write 5 [platform] captions for [business] about [topic]. Brand voice: [describe]. Each under [number] words, with a simple call to action and up to 3 hashtags. Avoid claims I can't prove.

=== 8. Spreadsheet helper ===
I use [Google Sheets / Excel]. My data: [describe columns, e.g. A = name, B = email, C = date]. I want to [goal]. Give me the formula, explain it in one line, and tell me which cell to put it in.
