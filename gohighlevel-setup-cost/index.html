Short answer: there are two costs. Your GoHighLevel subscription, paid to HighLevel every month, and the setup, paid once to whoever builds it. This guide breaks down both, with real price ranges for Ireland, the UK and Australia, and what a good setup should include.

1. The GoHighLevel subscription

HighLevel sells monthly plans directly. The entry plan covers one business account, and higher plans add unlimited sub-accounts and white-label features for agencies. AI features such as Voice AI and Conversation AI, plus phone, SMS and email sending, are billed on top by usage. Always check HighLevel's own pricing page for the current numbers, because plans and usage rates change.

Tip: if you're a single business, you rarely need the agency plans. Start on the entry plan and add AI features once your follow-up is working.

3. What a good GoHighLevel setup includes

  1. Pipeline and stages that match how you actually sell, from new enquiry to booked to won.
  2. Speed to lead: every new lead gets an instant SMS, email or AI reply.
  3. Calendar and reminders, with no-show and cancellation recovery.
  4. Missed call text back, so a missed call still becomes a conversation.
  5. Nurture and reactivation for leads who didn't book the first time.
  6. Tracking: Meta Pixel and Google Ads conversions sent from real leads, not page views.
  7. A2P 10DLC and domains set up properly so your texts and emails actually arrive.
  8. Documentation and a handover so your team can run it.

If a quote doesn't mention follow-up, tracking or a handover, you're probably paying for pages without the system behind them.

4. Freelancer vs agency

An agency gives you a team and an account manager, and charges for both. A specialist freelancer is usually cheaper and you talk to the person doing the work, but check they're certified, have reviews you can read, and can show results. Ask for examples of follow-up and tracking, not just nice pages.

Questions

Is GoHighLevel expensive?

The subscription is a monthly fee plus usage for AI, calls, SMS and email. For most small businesses it replaces several tools (CRM, email, SMS, booking, website builder), so the total is often lower than what they paid before.

How long does a GoHighLevel setup take?

A basic setup with a website, a landing page, pipeline and follow-up is usually live in about 2 weeks. Adding ads, tracking and lead lists takes about 3 to 4 weeks.

Can I set up GoHighLevel myself?

Yes, and many people start that way. Where it usually goes wrong is follow-up workflows, A2P registration, domains and tracking. If those aren't right, leads go quiet and you can't see what's working.

What's the difference between a snapshot and a custom setup?

A snapshot copies a pre-built account template into yours. It's fast, but it's built for someone else's business. A custom setup maps your offer, your lead sources and where leads drop off, then builds and tests every route.
